About

I treat reliability as a feature, not an afterthought. I have spent the last decade in regulated environments where failure is not an option: payment processing with PCI-DSS Level 1 certification, energy and industrial control systems, air-gapped networks.

The work I take: air-gapped, on-prem hybrid, multi-cloud, where the cluster is mine to run, not just a managed-service bill at the end of the month. Kubernetes, eBPF, and the boring repeatable workflows (SOPs) that keep complex systems running predictably.

I am not looking for work. This page exists because people ask for it. If you are reviewing my profile, the CV is on LinkedIn. If you are a recruiter, the contact section tells you how to reach me. If you are a friend, the GitHub link has my open-source work.
