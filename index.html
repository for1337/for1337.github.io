01 / About

Engineering for environments where mistakes are not recoverable.

I work on the kind of infrastructure where the cost of failure does not scale linearly — payment processing under PCI-DSS Level 1, energy and industrial control systems, air-gapped networks, company-wide estate hardening. Ten years in regulated environments, on call, hands on the keyboard, writing the runbook at 03:00.

The work I do is air-gapped, on-prem, hybrid, multi-cloud — where the cluster is mine to run, not just a managed-service bill at the end of the month. Kubernetes, eBPF, .NET / C#, and the boring repeatable workflows (SOPs) that keep complex systems running predictably.
